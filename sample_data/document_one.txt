The school library plans to extend its hours during exam week. Normally the library closes at 5 PM, but during the exam period it will remain open until 8 PM from Monday through Thursday. The change begins on December 7 and ends December 17. Students may use the study rooms, but groups must reserve rooms at the desk. The library will offer free printing for up to 10 pages per student each day. Staff said the new schedule is temporary and may change based on attendance. The library will close at its normal time on Friday.
