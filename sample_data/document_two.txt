The student garden club will start a composting project in the spring. The club plans to collect vegetable scraps from the cafeteria three days each week. The compost will be used in the school garden, which grows tomatoes and herbs. Volunteers will receive training before handling the bins. The club needs approval from the facilities manager before placing the bins outside. A small pilot will run for four weeks, after which organizers will record the amount collected and any odor issues. The project does not yet have a confirmed start date.
